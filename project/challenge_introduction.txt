Welcome to the challenge!
We offer three challenge modes for you to choose from.
Questions for each challenge are randomly selected from the challenge words library, a single round consists of five questions, and you must answer at least four correctly to succeed.
The game presents a Finnish word, and the player must provide its English meaning. A correct answer earns a point, while an incorrect answer allows you to view the correct meaning.
For "Level" and "Interest" challenges, you can choose to study the vocabulary list first or start the challenge immediately.
Good luck!
